# AI Search: 90-day editorial backlog (INTERNAL)

Each item answers one distinct buyer question, has a named primary reference, an honest date and a real author. Nothing is
published without a source for each claim. Two items a month is a realistic pace for a small team; the order is by buyer intent.

## Days 1 to 30: decision questions
1. What does an AI search audit actually test? (a worked, anonymised walk-through of the method; no client result unless agreed)
2. Does blocking GPTBot hide you from ChatGPT? (the provider documentation, explained, with a decision table)
3. AI Overviews and AI Mode in Search Console: where the traffic appears and what it cannot tell you (Google documentation)
4. How to disambiguate a business with a common or similar name

## Days 31 to 60: how-to
5. How to write an answer-first service page (a before-and-after with a fictional business)
6. What an entity fact sheet is and how to write one (template included)
7. How to ask customers for reviews honestly (what platforms prohibit; a template)
8. Directory listings: which kinds matter for a services firm, and how to keep them consistent

## Days 61 to 90: evidence
9. Our own baseline, before and after: what we changed on odoovations.com and what we observed (only what has actually been observed)
10. The mistakes we see in robots.txt, CDN and bot-protection settings (from reviews we have really done, anonymised)
11. Measuring AI search without fooling yourself: variability, sample size and API versus product results
12. Urdu and Roman Urdu questions: what we found when we tested them (only if tested)

## Rules
- Never invent a result, a statistic, a client or a quotation. If the evidence does not exist yet, the article waits.
- Update an article's date only when its substance changes.
- Link each article to the relevant service page and to two sibling guides.
