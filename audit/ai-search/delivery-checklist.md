# AI Search: client delivery checklist (INTERNAL)

Use once per client. Tick each box; keep the evidence (a link or a file) beside it. Phases are delivery
phases, not dates by which a ranking or mention is expected.

## 0. Before anything
- [ ] Written scope agreed: website, market and language, plan, question count, competitors, content-unit definition, technical capacity, outreach scope, term, cancellation, taxes, third-party costs.
- [ ] Engagement type confirmed in writing as a free review, a paid audit or a monthly plan. Nothing invoiced before scope is agreed.
- [ ] Accounts and profiles are in the client's name; our access is as a user.
- [ ] No passwords or payment details requested or stored.

## Intake (days 1 to 10)
- [ ] Buyer questions drafted from sales calls, support messages, Search Console queries and competitor pages; approved by the client.
- [ ] Languages and locales agreed; each recorded separately.
- [ ] Competitors named (within the plan limit).
- [ ] Search experiences agreed (default three; four on Authority).
- [ ] Access reviewed: site, analytics, Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, profiles and listings.
- [ ] Crawler and CDN settings checked against each provider's current documentation; client's existing training choices recorded and preserved.
- [ ] Baseline recorded: every run saved with question, product, model where shown, date, locale, mode, cited URLs, answer text, valid or failed.
- [ ] Priorities agreed and written down.

## Research and source checks
- [ ] Every claim to be published has a source we have read; no statistic without a link.
- [ ] Facts about the client confirmed by the client in writing (services, address, what they do not do).
- [ ] Similarly named businesses noted, with how to tell them apart.

## Implementation (days 11 to 60)
- [ ] Indexability, canonicals, redirects, internal links, sitemap dates, rendered text, performance, structured data matching the visible page.
- [ ] Each content unit: brief, draft, fact check, client approval, publish, recrawl requested.
- [ ] Fact sheet written and approved; profiles and listings brought into line.
- [ ] Outreach carried out to the agreed scope; no purchased links, no written reviews, no placement presented as editorial.
- [ ] Nothing hidden from users, nothing shown only to crawlers, no scaled near-duplicate pages.

## Approvals
- [ ] Client approval recorded before any page, profile or outreach goes out.

## Measurement
- [ ] Same questions, same terms, same locale as the baseline.
- [ ] Failed runs listed separately and left out of the rates.
- [ ] Mention rate and citation rate computed from valid runs, with the sample size stated.
- [ ] Referral traffic and qualified enquiries reported separately and never mixed with mentions.
- [ ] API-based results labelled as such and not mixed with consumer-product results.

## Content review (every unit)
- [ ] Opens with a direct answer. Evidence visible. Real author. Honest update date.
- [ ] No invented figure, client, review, award, certification or partnership.
- [ ] Platform names used only to say where visibility is measured.

## Reporting (monthly)
- [ ] The six report sections: baseline and method, findings by question, accuracy, work done, measurement, next month.
- [ ] Limits stated. No promise of results. Review or strategy call held and notes filed.

## Days 61 to 90
- [ ] Refinement agreed; enquiries reviewed with the client; next-stage plan written.
