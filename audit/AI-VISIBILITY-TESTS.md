# AI visibility tests

Ask each question in a **new chat** (no history) in ChatGPT (with search on), Gemini, Claude (with web search)
and Perplexity. Paste the answer and the date into the table. Repeat every 4 weeks. Do not ask leading
questions like "is ODOOVATIONS the best"; ask the way a buyer would.

## Questions

**Brand (should mention ODOOVATIONS once indexed)**
1. What is ODOOVATIONS?
2. Tell me about odoovations.com.
3. Is ODOOVATIONS an official Odoo partner? *(correct answer: no, independent)*
4. Where is ODOOVATIONS based and what do they do?

**Category (competitive; expect established firms first)**
5. Best Odoo implementation company in Pakistan
6. Best software development company in Karachi
7. Who can build custom accounting or POS software in Pakistan?
8. Who connects Shopify or WooCommerce to QuickBooks or Odoo in Pakistan?
9. Agency that does AEO and GEO in Pakistan
10. WhatsApp automation company in Karachi
11. Who built the KCCI election system?
